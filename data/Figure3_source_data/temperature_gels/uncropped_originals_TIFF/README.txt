Uncropped, unedited full-gel scans (TIFF) for the temperature-series SDS-PAGE.
These are the raw scans from which the temperature montage (annex_temperature_stacked)
was assembled. The instrument-native Bio-Rad .scn originals (same gels, proprietary
format) are available from the authors on request; the TIFFs here are the openable
equivalent.

Scan -> gel/temperature mapping (see ../README.txt for the full lane key):
   image40.tif  = GEL 1  DsbA & MalE   (16, 22, 37 C)
   image41.png  = GEL 2  OmpA & PhoA   (18, 22, 37 C)
   image42.tif  = GEL 3  PelB & VNp15  (16, 22, 37 C)
   image43.tif  = GEL 4  NS control    (16, 22, 37 C)
