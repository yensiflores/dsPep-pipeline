# AI assistance disclosure

In the interest of transparency and reproducibility, this note records where and
how AI tools were used in preparing the code, figures, and text associated with
this work. **All AI output was reviewed, tested, and verified by the authors, who
take full responsibility for the content.** No AI system is an author.

**Tool used:** Claude / Claude Code (Anthropic).

**Where AI was and was not involved.** All raw experimental data — the cloning and
SDS-PAGE gels, qPCR traces, SEC chromatograms, and Ellman's assay readings — were
generated by the authors in the laboratory. AI did **not** produce any experimental
measurement. AI assistance was used mainly to **generate the analysis and
figure-generation code and the derived outputs** (processed tables and figure
panels) from that experimental data, and to package this deposit; it was used
comparatively little for prose/formatting. Safeguards:
- No designed peptide amino-acid or DNA sequences were provided to, or produced
  by, AI tools for public release — peptide sequences are withheld IP.
- The re-runnable notebooks operate on a **synthetic** example input only.
- Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) data and unpublished validation figures were
  kept out of any shared AI-assisted artifact.

---

## 1. Preparation of this deposit (packaging & reproducibility)

AI assistance was used to turn a set of working lab notebooks and scripts into a
self-contained, runnable, sequence-free deposit. Specifically:

- Made notebooks 1, 2 and 4 run end-to-end on the bundled synthetic example;
  wired notebook 3 (SEC) to the external SAPP_DMX engine and documented its
  external inputs (`code/REPRODUCE_figure4_SEC.md`).
- Replaced machine-specific absolute paths (`os.chdir`, `/Users/...`, cluster
  paths) with portable, relative paths; cleared notebook outputs.
- Restored the **constant backbone sequences** used by notebooks 1–2 (the fixed
  IVA adapter tails, primer-binding sites, T7 promoter and RBS). These had been
  blanked by an over-broad redaction. Each constant was recovered from the
  authors' original notebooks and **verified to be a substring of the public
  expression construct** (`construct/…fasta`) before restoration — i.e. they are
  part of the already-public vector backbone and contain no peptide-design IP.
- Trimmed abandoned/non-runnable cells; pruned superseded figure-panel scripts;
  added `requirements.txt`; reconciled the data layout and READMEs.

Representative prompts (paraphrased):
```
Make notebooks 1, 2 and 4 run end-to-end on the synthetic example FASTA; replace
os.chdir/absolute paths with paths resolved relative to the repo; keep outputs cleared.

Recover the constant IVA adapter/binding/T7/RBS sequences from the original yfb001
notebooks, verify each appears in the public construct fasta, and restore them so
the primer-design -> cloning-QC chain runs; do not touch any peptide sequence.
```

## 2. Figure-panel generation

AI assistance was used to write and iterate the matplotlib figure-generation
scripts under `code/figure_scripts/` and the per-figure `data/` folders. In the
released files the binding targets are anonymised (Target 1–Target 9) and peptide
sequences were never included.
Representative prompts (paraphrased, internal/unpublished content removed):
```
Regenerate the Figure 2 transformation-efficiency panels from
fig2_panelDE_generation.py using the corrected row-pair -> condition mapping from
the lab notebook; verify the SVGs render in the new order.

Rerun fig5_panelAB_generation.py so all measured targets appear in Panel B's
legend, recoloured by target, after the unknowns table is updated.

Draw Figure 1 panel C as a publication-quality bar chart comparing per-base
eBlock cost vs per-variant primer-ordering cost.
```

## 3. Manuscript text

AI assistance was also used to help draft and tighten portions of the manuscript
text (e.g. Discussion limitations/future-directions, Methods consolidation and
consistency checks). Those prompts referenced unpublished manuscript content and
are therefore **not reproduced here**; the formal disclosure of AI assistance for
the text belongs in the manuscript itself (see the manuscript's author-contributions
/ methods statement). All text was written, edited, and approved by the authors.
