# AI assistance disclosure

In the interest of transparency and reproducibility, this note records where and
how AI tools were used in preparing the code, figures, and text associated with
this work. **All AI output was reviewed, tested, and verified by the authors, who
take full responsibility for the content.** No AI system is an author.

**Tool used:** Claude / Claude Code (Anthropic).

**Where AI was and was not involved.** All raw experimental data — the cloning and
SDS-PAGE gels, qPCR traces, SEC chromatograms, and Ellman's assay readings — were
generated by the authors in the laboratory. AI did **not** produce any experimental
measurement. The authors carried out the analysis and designed the figures; AI
assistance was used mainly to help **write and refine the analysis and
figure-generation code** and to package this deposit.

---

## 1. Preparation of this deposit (packaging & reproducibility)

AI assistance was used to turn a set of working lab notebooks and scripts into a
self-contained, runnable, sequence-free deposit. Specifically:

- Made notebooks 1, 2 and 4 run end-to-end on the bundled synthetic example;
  wired notebook 3 (SEC) to the external SAPP_DMX engine and documented its
  external inputs (`code/REPRODUCE_figure4_SEC.md`).
- Replaced machine-specific absolute paths (`os.chdir`, `/Users/...`, cluster
  paths) with portable, relative paths; cleared notebook outputs.
- Trimmed abandoned/non-runnable cells; pruned superseded figure-panel scripts;
  added `requirements.txt`; reconciled the data layout and READMEs.

Representative prompt (paraphrased):
```
Make notebooks 1, 2 and 4 run end-to-end on the synthetic example FASTA; replace
os.chdir/absolute paths with paths resolved relative to the repo; keep outputs cleared.
```

## 2. Figure-panel scripts

Working from the authors' figure designs and data, AI assistance was used to help
write and refine some of the matplotlib figure-generation scripts under
`code/figure_scripts/` and the per-figure `data/` folders. In the released files
the binding targets are anonymised (Target 1–Target 9) and peptide sequences were
never included.
Representative prompts (paraphrased, internal/unpublished content removed):
```
Rerun fig5_panelAB_generation.py so all measured targets appear in Panel B's
legend, recoloured by target, after the unknowns table is updated.
```

## 3. Manuscript text

The manuscript text was **written by the authors**; AI assistance was used for
editing the manuscript. Those prompts referenced unpublished manuscript content and
are therefore **not reproduced here**; the formal disclosure of AI assistance for
the text belongs in the manuscript itself (see its author-contributions / use-of-AI
statement). All text was written and approved by the authors.
